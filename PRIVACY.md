# Privacy Policy — gcal-bot

This bot is a private hobby project for a closed Telegram group, not a
commercial service.

## What data is processed

- **Calendar data**: title, time, and location of events from the configured
  Google Calendar iCal feed.
- **Telegram user data**: name (or username) and Telegram user ID of people
  who click Yes/No/Maybe.

## Where data is stored

Exclusively in a local SQLite database on the server running the bot
(operated by whoever runs that particular deployment). No data is shared with
third parties, no tracking, no analytics, no ads.

## Deletion

Past events (and their RSVP entries) are automatically removed once the event
has passed. Anyone who wants their RSVP history deleted can contact the
operator of the bot instance.

## Contact

Questions about this bot: [GitHub repo issues](https://github.com/christoph-teichmeister/gcal-bot/issues)
or directly the operator of the specific bot instance.
